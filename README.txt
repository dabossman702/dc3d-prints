DC3D PRINTS WEBSITE
====================

Files:
- index.html  - homepage
- style.css   - responsive styling
- assets/     - your uploaded product photos

HOW TO USE:
1. Open index.html in a browser to preview the site.
2. Upload the whole DC3D_Prints_Site folder to a static host such as Vercel, Netlify, or GitHub Pages.
3. The quote form is currently a front-end demo. Connect it to your email/form service before accepting real orders.
4. Replace/add photos in assets/ and update the product cards in index.html as your catalog grows.
